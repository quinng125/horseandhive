Questions

Boarding FAQ

Want a tour when a space opens? Call (443) 606-4639.

Do you have any boarding spaces available?

Boarding is full right now. Join the waitlist and we'll contact you as soon as a space opens.

What's included in field board?

Feeding up to one scoop twice daily, water trough checks, and 24-hour access to a run-in shed.

What's the difference between field and stall board?

Stall board adds stalling on bad-weather days, with hay and water in the stall and daily stall cleaning. Special-care and 24-hour stall options are available for horses that need more.

Can I bring my own trainer?

Yes. Boarders can take lessons with our trainers or bring in their own insured trainer.

Is there somewhere to ride in bad weather?

Yes, we have a small indoor for bad-weather riding, plus two lighted outdoor arenas for evenings.

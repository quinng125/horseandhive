How we teach

Confident riders start on the ground, with the right horse and an instructor who's paying attention.

We keep our lesson program intimate, so our trained and certified instructors can teach every student every part of riding. Every new student starts with a private lesson to review the farm rules, assess their level and match them with the right horse and lesson format.

  • Beginner through advanced, from a first walk to jumping courses
  • New riders to show riders, with instructors experienced in jumping, dressage and eventing, plus gaited riding on our Tennessee Walker
  • Ages 5 and up, with plenty of adult riders too
  • Six days a week: Monday and Wednesday through Sunday, with a small indoor for bad weather
  • Happy lesson horses: each one gets at least one rest day every week

What to wear

Dress for the barn, not the mall.

Safety first, always. Here's everything a rider needs for their first lesson.

  • An approved helmet for every ride. Don't have one yet? We'll lend you one.
  • Boots with a heel, which keep feet safely in the stirrup. No sneakers or sandals.
  • Long pants or chaps to protect legs from rubbing. Jeans or riding tights are fine to start.
  • Arrive a little early. Lessons start on the hour, so give yourself time to meet your horse.

Questions

Lesson FAQ

Can't find your answer? Call (443) 606-4639 or send us a note.

Does my child need any riding experience?

None at all. Every new student starts with an introductory lesson, whatever their experience, so we can match them with the right horse and instructor.

How old do riders need to be?

We teach riders from 5 years old and up, and we have plenty of adult students too. Summer camp is for ages 6 and up.

What styles of riding do you teach?

Our instructors bring experience across hunter/jumper, dressage, eventing and western events, and Dorito, our Tennessee Walking Horse, teaches gaited riding. Tell us your goals and we'll match you with the right instructor.

When are lessons?

Lessons run Monday and Wednesday through Sunday, scheduled around availability and your needs. Spots are limited, so get in touch early. For a first lesson, text or call (443) 606-4639; returning students schedule directly with their instructor.

What happens if it rains?

When possible, instructors may teach inside in bad weather; each instructor decides, so check with yours. We'll always let you know if a lesson needs to move.

What's your cancellation policy?

Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel. Without it, the full lesson amount is still due. Payment is due on the day of your lesson.

Can I bring my own horse?

Boarders can take lessons with our trainers or bring in their own insured trainer. See boarding for details.

Welcome to the farm

We care for every horse as if it were our own, and we teach every rider the same way.

Tucked into the rolling country between Baltimore and Harford County, Horse & Hive offers riding lessons, boarding and a variety of other programs in a professional, safe environment.

Our lesson horses each have their own diet, health and training plan, and every one of them gets at least one rest day a week. Happy horses make confident riders.

Your first ride

Three easy steps to the saddle.

Every new student starts with an introductory lesson, whatever their experience, so we can pair you with the right horse and the right instructor from day one.

  1. Say hello

    Tell us about the rider: age, experience and what they'd love to learn. We'll find a time for an introductory lesson.

  2. Gear up

    Long pants or chaps, boots with a heel and an approved riding helmet. No helmet yet? We'll lend you one.

  3. Meet your horse

    Lessons run an hour and start on the hour, with an instructor matched to your level, from first walk to first canter and beyond.

The hive in Horse & Hive

Eggs from our own hens. Honey from our own bees.

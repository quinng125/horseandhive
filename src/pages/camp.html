About camp

A summer they'll talk about all year, whether it's their first time near a horse or their fiftieth.

  • Ages 6 and up. Campers must be fully potty-trained.
  • All experience levels welcome. No riding experience needed.

Packing list

What to bring each day.

Label everything! Barn days are busy, happy and occasionally muddy.

  1. Ride-ready clothes

    Closed-toe, low-heeled boots, long pants and a shirt for riding. No shorts, sneakers or sandals in the saddle.

  2. Swim & spare kit

    A swimsuit and towel every day, plus an extra set of clothes.

  3. Lunch, snacks & water

    Pack lunch, snacks and a water bottle daily. We're a nut-free farm, so no nut products, please.

Questions

Camp FAQ

Still wondering? Call {{phone}} or send us a note.

Does my child need riding experience?

No. Camp welcomes all experience levels. The one exception is advanced week, which is for riders already confident at walk, trot and canter.

How does advanced week work?

Campers need to be proficient at the walk, trot and canter. We'll do a quick skills assessment on the first day to make sure everyone's in the right group.

What's the minimum age?

Campers must be at least 6 years old and fully potty-trained. We've also run an adult camp day for grown-ups.

Can we book single days?

Yes. Alongside full weeks we offer full-day ($150) and half-day ($115) options.

When is payment due?

All camp fees are due in full at sign-up.

Are nuts allowed?

No. Horse & Hive is a nut-free farm, so please pack lunches and snacks without nuts.

How we teach

Confident riders start on the ground, with the right horse and an instructor who's paying attention.

Our instructors all bring years of experience or formal certification, including CHA (Certified Horsemanship Association) credentials. Every new student, whatever their background, starts with an introductory lesson so we can match them to the right horse, level and lesson format.

  • Beginner through advanced, from a first walk to jumping courses
  • English & Western, plus gaited riding on our Tennessee Walker
  • Ages 5 and up, with plenty of adult riders too
  • Six days a week, weather permitting, with a small indoor for rainy days
  • Happy lesson horses: each one gets at least one rest day every week

What to wear

Dress for the barn, not the mall.

Safety first, always. Here's everything a rider needs for their first lesson.

  • An approved helmet

    Required for every ride. Don't have one yet? We'll lend you one.

  • Boots with a heel

    A small heel keeps feet safely in the stirrup. No sneakers or sandals.

  • Long pants or chaps

    Protects legs from rubbing. Jeans or riding tights are fine to start.

  • Arrive a little early

    Lessons start on the hour, so give yourself time to meet your horse.

Questions

Lesson FAQ

Can't find your answer? Call {{phone}} or send us a note.

Does my child need any riding experience?

None at all. Every new student starts with an introductory lesson, whatever their experience, so we can match them with the right horse and instructor.

How old do riders need to be?

We teach riders from 5 years old and up, and we have plenty of adult students too. Summer camp is for ages 6 and up.

Do you teach English or Western?

Both. We also teach gaited riding on Dorito, our Tennessee Walking Horse.

What happens if it rains?

Lessons are weather permitting. We have a small indoor for bad-weather days, and we'll always let you know if we need to reschedule.

What's your cancellation policy?

Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel. Without it, the full lesson amount is still due. Payment is due on the day of your lesson.

Can I bring my own horse?

Boarders can take lessons with our trainers or bring in their own insured trainer. See boarding for details.

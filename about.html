Our farm

A small farm with a big heart.

Horses, bees, chickens and the people who look after them, at the corner of Long Green Pike and Hydes Road.

Our story

Why Horse & Hive? Because the horses aren't the only ones who live here.

Horse & Hive is a beautifully unique, family-owned farm in Hydes, Maryland, run by Amber and Ryan Frost. We offer riding lessons, boarding, camps and clinics in a professional, safe environment, close to both Baltimore and Harford County.

Our bees are part of how we got our name. When the season allows, we harvest honey from our own hives and collect eggs from our own chickens, and share them with the riders and families who make this place what it is.

Above all, we care for every horse as if it were our own. That shapes everything, from each lesson horse's diet and training plan to the rest day every horse gets each week.

What we believe

Three things we never compromise on.

  • Horses come first

    Individual diet, health and training programs for every horse, and at least one rest day a week. Happy horses teach better.

  • Safety, always

    Approved helmets on every ride, proper footwear, a private first lesson to review farm rules, and trained, certified instructors.

  • Every rider belongs

    From five-year-olds to adults, and from shy first-timers to riders who want to compete: there's a place for you here.

The team

Experienced instructors, endlessly patient.

We keep the lesson program intimate, so our trained and certified instructors can teach every student every part of riding, safely and with a lot of fun.

  • Instructor & trainer

    Lina

    Lina started riding at 6 and was working with trainers by 9, learning to build a partnership through ground work and saddle time. She has trained with many professionals, including an Olympic rider and a specialist in horse-and-rider biomechanics, and studied horsemanship with Jim McDonald.

  • Instructor

    Merideth

    Riding since her teens across hunter/jumper, dressage, eventing and even ranch sorting. Merideth has taught for 10 years and loves watching students progress and fall in love with horses.

  • Instructor

    Victoria

    Victoria's teaching centers on the fundamentals of equitation, drawing on her experience across disciplines. Her goal for every student: a strong, safe and confident rider.

Meet the herd

The real teachers have four legs.

Twelve lesson horses and ponies, from off-the-track Thoroughbreds to Haflingers, a Paint, a Quarter Horse, a Tennessee Walker, a POA and some wonderful crosses. Every one has its own diet, health and training plan.

  • Archie

    Age 8

    Thoroughbred (OTTB)

    A retired racehorse who has proven kind and willing on the ground and under saddle. Archie teaches beginner through advanced lessons, loves ground work and has a goofy, fun personality.

  • Cannoli

    Age 7

    Haflinger

    A newer member of the lesson program who is already a student favorite, thanks to his silly personality and flowing mane.

  • Cloud

    Age 20

    Paint

    The dictionary definition of a perfect lesson horse: reliable, kind and a gentleman, with a competitive streak that shines in jumping and pony games.

  • Dorito

    Age 19

    Tennessee Walking Horse

    Easy to handle on the ground, and the horse who teaches students what a gaited horse is and how to ride one.

  • Mater

    Age 13

    Thoroughbred (OTTB)

    Another OTTB and a true “unicorn”: a little quirky, very forgiving of rider mistakes, and great for beginners through advanced riders.

  • Mani

    Age 12

    Quarter Horse

    Manicotti is friendly on the ground and a challenge under saddle that makes students better riders. He has done a bit of everything, including jumping.

  • Meatball

    Age 19

    Arab cross

    Just as silly as her name implies: a little round ball of fun whose favorite things are snacks and naps.

  • Mocha

    Age 16

    Draft cross

    Sweet and sensitive. A little shy at first, but once a rider earns his trust it’s forever. Mocha can even be ridden bridleless.

  • Peaches

    Age 19

    Haflinger

    A gem of a lesson pony for our youngest riders and advanced students alike. Spunky, with opinions of her own!

  • Sprite

    Age 21

    Pony of the Americas

    Been there, done that, eventing included. The smallest member of the team makes up for it with a spunky attitude.

  • Sundae

    Age 31

    Arab cross

    Our oldest team member, but don’t tell her that. A former ranch horse who is full of energy and now enjoys dressage and jumping.

  • Tater Tot

    Age 21

    Haflinger

    Missing an eye and an ear, and still a stellar lesson pony, beloved for her sweet and kind nature. Truly one of a kind.

Honey & eggs

Hens, bees & the hive in Horse & Hive.

When available, we offer fresh honey and eggs harvested right here on the farm, from our own bees and our own chickens. Batches are small and seasonal, so availability varies.

More at the farm

There's always something going on.

Beyond lessons and camp, the farm hosts programs and events through the year. Ask us what's coming up next.

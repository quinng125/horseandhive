Questions

Frequently asked.

More answers on the lessons, boarding and camp pages.

How do I book a first lesson?

Call (443) 606-4639 or send an enquiry above. Every new student starts with an introductory lesson, and we recommend it be a private one.

Can I just drop by to look around?

We're a working farm, so please call ahead. We'd be happy to find a good time for you to visit.

What ages do you teach?

Riders from 5 years old through adult. Summer camp is for ages 6 and up.

Do I need my own helmet?

An approved riding helmet is required, but if you don't have one yet, we'll lend you one.

Do you sell honey and eggs?

When available! Both are harvested on the farm from our own bees and chickens, so supplies are seasonal and limited. Ask us what's in stock.
